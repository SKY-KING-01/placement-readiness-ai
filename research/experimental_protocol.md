# Protocol for future student pilots

Use consented, de-identified data under the institution’s ethics and privacy process. Fix metrics, analysis, sample size, intervention duration and exclusions before enrollment. Keep selection of training cohorts separate from any institutional recruitment decision.

## Validate role-weighted prioritization

Have independent role reviewers rate the importance of skills and review the top three gap recommendations. Compare agreement and ranking quality with an unweighted gap baseline within each role. Measure topic coverage and disagreement between reviewers. Keep assessment scoring fixed during the comparison.

## Adaptive practice pilot

Randomize consenting students between an adaptive question sequence and fixed practice. Stratify by initial standardized readiness and assigned role. Equalize practice opportunity and question exposure. Use change on an unseen standardized reassessment as the primary outcome. Track adherence, time on task, difficulty and item exposure as secondary measures. Analyze by assignment and report attrition in both groups.

## Learning roadmap pilot

Compare a policy-grounded OpenAI roadmap with the deterministic plan, using the same underlying assessments, study-time budget, curated catalog and reassessment. Blind assessors to assignments where feasible. Track reassessment score, completion, and independent ratings of accuracy and usefulness. Predefine safety checks for incorrect resources and advice. Treat power and subgroup sizes as constraints on interpretation.

## Prediction validation

Collect a wider range of standardized reassessments and independently observed outcomes. Use student-disjoint enrollment periods, account for censoring, and compare against last-score and simpler classifiers. Evaluate calibration, subgroup errors and matching between historical and future assessment blueprints. Use a locked test set for final reporting.
