# Research limits

The 100-person cohort and its improvements are synthetic. Model errors describe a simulation, including assumptions in the generator. They cannot support claims about real placement probability, learning effectiveness, causal effects or fairness. The prototype readiness threshold is an instructional target.

The classifier has only three validation positives and 15 held-out students. Its chosen cutoff has a substantial false-positive count. Estimation error is therefore high and calibration evidence is weak. Its comparison cannot be made strictly calendar-forward with these baseline dates and this follow-up window. The primary regression test includes repeated outcomes for 15 people, and a second calendar-forward test has 15 outcomes. Regression subgroup reporting is sparse by design.

Historical baseline, simulated and live assessment blueprints have different item evidence. The default app suppresses synthetic-trained estimates for real learner records. A facilitated synthetic demo exposes suitable scores and includes provenance status. The approved-format question bank still needs subject-matter validation and assessment comparability work. The design includes an adaptive heuristic without claiming fitted item response theory.

SHAP and causal interpretation are deferred. With this small cohort, model explanation would describe the simulation more than real students. The project defines empirical pilots for adaptive practice and roadmaps with no claims that they have been run.
